## Post

A lot of people asked to try Toneword after yesterday's post, so — you can. It runs in the browser right now.

Plug your guitar into an audio interface, or just load a clip. Six sliders — Warmth, Bite, Air, Body, Glass, Velvet — and a parametric EQ does the math underneath.

The thing I keep coming back to is the frequency panel. It shows you the actual EQ curve as you move the sliders, so you start building intuition about what "warm" actually means at the frequency level. Click the Midnight Jazz preset and watch velvet push everything dark and smooth. Click Nashville Twang and see where bite lives on the spectrum. It's a teaching tool disguised as an effect.

No signup, no install, no data collection — it's just a local Web Audio app that runs in your browser. Still early, still evolving. VST version is next.

Link in the comments if you want to try it.
